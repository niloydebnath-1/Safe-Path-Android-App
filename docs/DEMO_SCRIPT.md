# 3–5 Minute Demo Script

## 0:00–0:30 — Problem and solution

“People often share crime warnings or road hazards on social media, but the information is scattered and rarely reaches the correct authority. Nirapod turns those posts into location-based, trackable reports.”

## 0:30–1:40 — Citizen report

1. Login as Demo Citizen.
2. Open Safety Map and show existing hotspot markers.
3. Open Report a Hazard.
4. Capture or show a hazard photo.
5. Capture GPS location.
6. Write a short Bangla/English description.
7. Run Gemini AI analysis.
8. Show category, severity, risk, authority and summary.
9. Submit the report.

## 1:40–2:20 — Citizen tracking and verification

1. Open My Reports.
2. Show Submitted status.
3. Open another report and press community confirmation.
4. Explain that repeated confirmations strengthen confidence without making AI the final decision.

## 2:20–3:20 — Authority workflow

1. Logout and login as Demo Authority.
2. Open Authority Dashboard.
3. Show total/open/high-risk metrics.
4. Open the new report.
5. Change status from Submitted to Received, Verified, In Progress and Resolved.

## 3:20–4:00 — SOS and warning

1. Login as citizen and long-press SOS.
2. Return to Authority Dashboard and show the active location alert.
3. On the map, press current location; when a high-risk report is within 300 metres, show the nearby caution message.

## Closing

“Nirapod creates one shared digital channel from citizen evidence to authority action, with AI assistance, community verification and transparent status tracking.”
